No wallet in this browser. A wallet is two post-quantum keys: a hot key that signs here
(65,536 one-time keys) and a cold recovery key you keep offline. Your address is fixed by
both when the wallet is made. The keys never leave this browser. The hot key is stored
encrypted under a passphrase you choose here; nobody can reset it for you.
