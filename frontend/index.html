WALLET 









  
No wallet in this browser. A wallet is two post-quantum keys: a hot key that signs here
(65,536 one-time keys, and a series of further keys from the same seed) and a cold recovery
key you keep offline. Your address is fixed by both when the wallet is made. The keys never
leave this browser. The hot key is stored encrypted under a passphrase you choose here;
nobody can reset it for you. (Lost the hot key of an address? See COLD KEY, below.)

COLD KEY




 



  
The cold key is the final authority over an address. It can freeze the address at once (its
hot key then signs nothing), cancel a rotation the hot key started, unfreeze, and replace the
hot key with a new one made here. Whoever holds it can do all of that, which is why it lives
offline, on paper. It is used once per action, in this page's memory, and never stored: paste
it again for each action.

  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
RECOVER: replace the address's hot key with a new one made here. It takes effect a delay of
blocks after the block that carries it. On an address that is not frozen, whoever holds the
hot key can cancel it until then. If the hot key may be stolen and not just lost, freeze
first: on a frozen address the hot key cannot cancel the recovery.
