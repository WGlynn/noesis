# Noesis — one page

**Proof of Mind: a value chain for verified contribution.**

---

**The line.** Bitcoin makes you spend electricity to take part — that cost keeps spam and fakes out. Noesis gets the same protection from genuine contribution instead, and pays you for it — provably: copies earn nothing, and money alone can't finalize. Telling real contribution from a well-made fake is the open problem, named below.

**Why it's new.** Bitcoin built the missing piece that let a market for *trustless ownership* exist. Noesis builds the missing piece that lets a market for *contribution* exist. Same kind of leap, one market over.

**The core move.** The chain becomes its own market. It doesn't ask a committee or a formula what your work is worth — it lets the market reveal it the way every market does: by **what later work actually builds on.** Useful work gets built on and earns; dead ends don't.

**What you earn.** Each contribution is fingerprinted, priced by its real downstream impact, and split into two things:
- a **soulbound standing** (Proof of Mind — reputation + voting weight you earn and can't sell), and
- a **transferable storage credit** you can trade.
> You can buy storage; you cannot buy consensus.

**Why it's hard to game.** One rule — *pay only for novel work others build on* — handles the attacks in our tests: copies earn zero, spam saturates at a ceiling, collusion rings are just value moving in a circle (and the chain sees it), and the learned judge can only lower a score, never invent one. The hard case is a group of real, separate accounts building on each other's worthless work without a circle: the record alone can't tell that apart from real collaboration. Closing it needs signals from outside the graph (independence that costs something to fake, and a usefulness test the contributor can't move); that work is designed and partly measured, not built.

**Two big claims.**
1. **Cooperation by construction** — cheating isn't punished, it just doesn't pay. The honest move is the highest-paying one, at every layer.
2. **A non-zero-sum chain, one node among many** — useful work from any chain is *conserved* onto one shared graph (a reverse fork = accretion), so a chain that reconciles keeps everything it built. The adoption war isn't won, it's dissolved. To every other chain: *not "come compete" — "come join us."* (Conservation core built and tested; cross-chain adapter designed, not yet built.)

**Claimable attribution** (the adoption engine). Provenance is a fact keyed to an identifier you already have, not a wallet — so the whole existing contribution graph is attributed *before* anyone joins. You don't earn from zero; you *claim what's already provably yours*. Consent-first: inert until claimed, right to disclaim, no payout without your key.

**Consensus.** Finality takes two keys: contribution carries two thirds of the vote and stake one third, each counted as a share of its own side, and each side must bring at least half of its own weight. So money can't finalize without contributors, and contributors can't finalize without stake. At genesis, before any contribution has vested, stake finalizes alone. Mining stays off the finality path: it orders blocks, prices fake accounts, and makes the money. Neither mining nor staking earns standing. Proof-of-work isn't wasted — it also runs a separate **energy money**: a proportional, Ergon-style coin priced by real energy, made to stay stable and be spent. PoM (scarce, unbuyable) carries most of the vote; the energy money (elastic) circulates. Each tool does only its job — using PoW is a strength, not a weakness.

**Fair launch.** The builder's private head-start is burned to zero at launch, verifiable on-chain. Everyone starts even.

**Honest status.** Built and tested in an open reference node: ownership, pricing, novelty, anti-spam/anti-ring defenses, dispute-and-slash, PoM-weighted finality. The one open problem — judging real-world contribution un-gameably at scale — we name as load-bearing and unsolved, with a judge grown from work that has already settled and frozen before each round, so the work it scores can't bend it, rather than a fixed formula that gets gamed on day one.
